Make Them Last

Nail Care Guide

How to clean, store, reuse and remove your press-ons for the longest wear.

🧼 Cleaning

Gently wipe with a soft cloth or cotton pad. Avoid soaking in water or scrubbing with harsh chemicals — this can damage the design and adhesive.

📦 Storage

Keep your set in the original storage box between wears, away from direct sunlight and heat, to protect the finish.

♻️ Reuse

With gentle handling, most sets can be reworn several times — reapply with fresh adhesive tabs or nail glue each time.

💧 Avoid Excess Water

Wear gloves for dishwashing or cleaning, and pat dry quickly after handwashing to extend wear time.

🪄 Proper Removal

Soak in warm water for a few minutes to loosen the adhesive, then gently lift from the side — never force or pull directly upward.

🎁 Storage Box Instructions

Each set comes in a labeled box — keep nails in size order for easy reapplication next time.
